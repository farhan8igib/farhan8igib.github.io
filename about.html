Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the College of Health & Life Sciences at Hamad Bin Khalifa University, where I founded Qatar's first experimental behavioural neuroscience research centre.

My research brings together biotechnology, neuroscience and computation to investigate neurological and psychiatric disorders. I want to understand how genetic and molecular changes reach up into neural circuits and shape what an animal does.

The lab pairs human cohorts with functional disease models and quantitative behaviour. Schizophrenia, depression, autism, cerebral palsy and Parkinson's disease sit at the centre of the work. The long-term aim is simple to state and hard to reach: find the mechanisms and biomarkers that make treatment more precise.

Before Doha, I trained across several labs and countries, from a PhD at IGIB in Delhi to postdoctoral work at Oxford, A*STAR in Singapore, and Duke-NUS. That path shaped a habit I still keep, which is to let the biological question choose the method rather than the other way round.
