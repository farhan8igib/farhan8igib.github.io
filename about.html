BIOGRAPHY

I am an Associate Professor in the College of Health & Life Sciences at Hamad Bin Khalifa University.

My research integrates biotechnology, neuroscience and computational approaches to investigate neurological and psychiatric disorders. I am particularly interested in understanding how genetic and molecular changes influence neural circuits and behavior.

Our work combines human cohorts with functional disease models and quantitative behavioral approaches. The long-term goal is to identify mechanisms and biomarkers that can support more precise approaches to neurological disease.
