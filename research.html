Research

Questions first.
Technology in service of biology.

Four questions, one shared method: connect a molecular change to something measurable in a living system.

Behavioural neuroscience

Reading behaviour, quantitatively

Precise, high-throughput approaches to learning, memory, sleep, sensorimotor gating and social behaviour, mostly in Drosophila.

Human genomics & multi-omics

Signatures of disease

Genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic and epigenetic signals of neurological and psychiatric disorders in Gulf and South Asian cohorts.

Functional neurogenetics

From candidate gene to circuit

Using fly models and optogenetics to test what a gene actually does to neural circuits and behaviour, not just what it correlates with.

AI & precision medicine

Making the data usable

Machine learning to prioritise biomarkers, characterise disease biology and speed up the search for therapeutics.
